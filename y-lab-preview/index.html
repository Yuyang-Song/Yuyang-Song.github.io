03 / ABOUT

Intelligence,
under construction.

Y-Lab is a research group at The University of Texas at Austin led by Yizhong Wang. We work on the systems, algorithms, and evaluation methods needed to build the next generation of language models and agents.

Our current interests span frontier-model training, learning from experience, multi-agent coordination, and long-horizon intelligence.
